Artificial Intelligence in Everyday Life

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that allows computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. It is now used in many areas of daily life.

1. AI in Education

AI can help students learn through personalized learning systems. These systems can suggest learning materials based on a student's progress and can provide quick feedback.

2. AI in Healthcare

In healthcare, AI can assist doctors by analyzing medical information and helping identify patterns in images and test results. It can support professionals, but important medical decisions still require qualified human judgment.

3. AI in Daily Applications

People interact with AI through voice assistants, recommendation systems, navigation applications, spam filters, and translation tools. These applications can make everyday tasks faster and more convenient.

Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence is becoming an important part of modern technology. When it is designed and used responsibly, it can support people in education, healthcare, communication, and many other areas.

Paragraph vs. Line Break

A new paragraph should be created with the <p> element when the text represents a separate idea or meaningful block of content.

A line break should be created with <br> only when a new line is needed without starting a new paragraph, such as in an address or a poem. Multiple <br> elements should not be used to create paragraph spacing or structure.
